Individual project for PLC Programming (ITLCA3) — ladder-logic control programs written and simulated in CODESYS, with the supporting electrical design.

- PWM control of a 300 W / 220 V cartridge heater in ladder logic: carrier generation with timers and up/down counters, comparator output, and a relay driven through an NPN transistor stage
- Sized the switching circuit from first principles — heater resistance and full-load current, transistor and relay selection with datasheet specs
- Egg incubator controller: start/stop latch with emergency stop, real-time-clock based cycle tracking (days / hours / minutes / seconds), fan, heat and humidifier outputs, cycle-end alarm and manual time adjustment
- HMI visualisation for the incubator with power, e-stop, reset, time set and humidity / temperature setpoints
- Thermistor temperature measurement in ladder logic using the Beta-parameter equation, converting a voltage-divider reading to °C
