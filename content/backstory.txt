Heading: My Story

I'm an only child, born in South Africa to a teacher and an engineering technician, and raised on one rule above all others: nothing comes from a handout. If I wanted something, I earned it. That, and the idea that you don't quit, have carried me through everything since.

I was seriously ill as a baby — ill enough that a doctor once told my mother I wouldn't make it through the night. My parents moved us to London so I could see specialists, and we came home when I was five. I started at Holy Cross Primary in Bellville, and in Grade 5 three things happened that shaped me: I won the AMESA National Maths Challenge, earned honours in my UNISA piano exams, and discovered table tennis.

Table tennis took over. I went from Western Province champion to South African national champion at 14, travelled the world with the national team, and balanced all of that with school at CBC St John's — tutors on tour, training camps every holiday, and a mother who made it clear that sport would never come at the expense of my grades.

At 16, just after competing in Vietnam, I was diagnosed with a brain tumour. Everything stopped. It took months of treatment, the support of my family and a small group of friends I still have today, and years of remission before life felt like mine again. I don't lead with it, and I don't want pity for it — but it's the reason I don't quit, and it's the reason I care about being useful.

I went on to spend seven years at Stellenbosch University in BEng Mechatronics, working around treatment and setbacks and finishing 95% of the degree before COVID-19 disrupted the final practical, lab-based components and took the last stretch away. I then completed a BSc IT (Robotics) at Eduvos and am now doing a BSc(Hons) in Data Science.

What I love most sits where mechanical, electrical and software engineering overlap. I've programmed PLCs and designed control logic for automated packaging lines, repaired inverters and drones on a QA bench, run CNC mills on a factory floor, and assisted with HVAC and fire-control system design. Solving, building, fixing — that's the work I want to do.
